|`flush_unissued_instr_i` | in | Prevent from issuing | CONTROLLER | logic

|`flush_i` | in | Flush whole scoreboard | CONTROLLER | logic

|`x_transaction_accepted_i` | in | TO_BE_COMPLETED | ISSUE_READ_OPERANDS | logic

|`x_issue_writeback_i` | in | TO_BE_COMPLETED | ISSUE_READ_OPERANDS | logic

|`x_id_i` | in | TO_BE_COMPLETED | ISSUE_READ_OPERANDS | logic[CVA6Cfg.TRANS_ID_BITS-1:0]

|`commit_instr_o` | out | Instructions to commit | COMMIT_STAGE | scoreboard_entry_t[CVA6Cfg.NrCommitPorts-1:0]

|`commit_drop_o` | out | Instruction is cancelled | COMMIT_STAGE | logic[CVA6Cfg.NrCommitPorts-1:0]

|`commit_ack_i` | in | Commit acknowledge | COMMIT_STAGE | logic[CVA6Cfg.NrCommitPorts-1:0]

|`decoded_instr_i` | in | Handshake's data with decode stage | ID_STAGE | scoreboard_entry_t[CVA6Cfg.NrIssuePorts-1:0]

|`issue_ack_i` | in | Issue stage acknowledge | ISSUE_READ_OPERANDS | logic[CVA6Cfg.NrIssuePorts-1:0]

|`fwd_o` | out | Forwarding | ISSUE_READ_OPERANDS | forwarding_t

|`resolved_branch_i` | in | Result from branch unit | EX_STAGE | bp_resolve_t

|`wt_valid_i` | in | Indicates valid results | EX_STAGE | logic[CVA6Cfg.NrWbPorts-1:0]

|`x_we_i` | in | Cvxif we for writeback | EX_STAGE | logic

|`x_rd_i` | in | CVXIF destination register | ISSUE_STAGE | logic[4:0]

Due to cv32a65x configuration, some ports are tied to a static value. These ports do not appear in the above table, they are listed below

As IsRVFI = 0,::
